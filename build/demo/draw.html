Lisp feels a bit back-to-front and inside-out when you first start!

Our Rocket is the last item to be processed, and our BACKGROUND variable the first. How To Design Programs is a great place to learn programming fundamentals.

__apf-3.2.7::lisp __apf-3.2.7::demo testing

Racket processes functions from the inside → out. Lisp languages calculate the inner-most function (data or variable) FIRST. It then works inside-out to render our ROCKET. That’s very maths-like. Try the first two chapters of HTDP, then try Elm Lang!
